# Clips' tests, by feature

- `making.rs`: making clips, one or several, of a VOD or an edit.
- `encoding.rs`: what a clip is made as, and made again as.
- `queue.rs`: the order clips are made in, canceling them, and their place in the jobs list.
- `files.rs`: deleting clips and cutting them from a bar, and the files they leave.
- `drag.rs`: dragging clips out of the window.
- `marks.rs`: cuts, chapters and highlights.
