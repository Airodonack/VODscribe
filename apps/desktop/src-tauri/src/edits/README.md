# Edits' tests

- `tests.rs`: the files in `tests/` that hold the specs of `edits.rs`.
- `tests/commands.rs`: making and changing edits: new edits and their names, opening one, its transcript and captions, putting things in, moving, trimming, splitting and taking out pieces and stretches, renaming and deleting.
- `tests/render.rs`: rendering an edit, stopping its render, and its video shown, dragged out and pictured.
